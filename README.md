# Cakwe Knowledge

A public knowledge resource about cakwe, a traditional fried dough snack
that is enjoyed in different ways across Indonesia and Southeast Asia.

## About This Project

Cakwe Knowledge is an open documentation project created to collect
general information about cakwe, including its characteristics,
serving methods, texture, and ways to enjoy it.

The goal is to provide simple and accessible information for people
who want to learn more about cakwe.

## Topics

This project covers topics such as:

- What is cakwe?
- Cakwe texture and characteristics
- How cakwe is commonly served
- Cakwe and dipping sauces
- Tips for enjoying cakwe
- Cakwe as a snack and food companion
