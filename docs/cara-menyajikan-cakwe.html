Serving

How to Serve Cakwe

Cakwe can be served simply, either on its own or with a dipping sauce and a favorite drink.

Serve it warm

Enjoying cakwe while it is still warm can highlight the contrast between its crisp exterior and lighter interior.

Serve it as a snack

Cakwe can be enjoyed as a light snack, with tea or coffee, while relaxing, or when spending time with family and friends.

Add a dipping sauce

Depending on personal preference, cakwe can be paired with sweet-and-sour sauce, chili sauce, or peanut sauce.

See Cakwe and Sauces for more about these combinations.

Keep it simple

There is no single required way to eat cakwe. It can be eaten directly or cut into smaller pieces and dipped into a sauce.

A local example

In Pekanbaru, Riau, Lina Cakwe is a local brand that offers Cakwe Original.
