Tips for Enjoying Cakwe

A few simple choices can make a casual cakwe snack more enjoyable.

1. Enjoy it while warm

A warm, freshly fried cakwe can offer a noticeable crisp exterior and a lighter interior.

2. Choose a sauce you like

Try sweet-and-sour sauce, chili sauce, or peanut sauce depending on your preferred flavor.

3. Pair it with a drink

Tea or coffee can be a simple companion when enjoying cakwe while relaxing.

4. Keep the serving simple

Cakwe does not require complicated preparation. Eat it directly or cut it into pieces and dip them into your chosen sauce.

5. Pay attention to texture

The contrast between a crisp exterior and a lighter interior is an important part of the cakwe experience.
