Cakwe basics

What Is Cakwe?

Cakwe is a fried dough snack with a crisp exterior and a lighter, airy interior.

Basic characteristics

Cakwe is made from a dough that is fried until the outside develops a golden-brown surface. The inside is generally lighter and more open in texture.

  • Golden-brown exterior
  • Crisp outside
  • Lighter, airy interior
  • Enjoyable when served warm

How is cakwe served?

Cakwe can be eaten on its own or paired with dipping sauces. Common choices can include sweet-and-sour sauce, chili sauce, or peanut sauce.

Cakwe as a snack

Its simple presentation makes cakwe suitable for casual snacking, relaxing, or enjoying alongside drinks such as tea or coffee.

Cakwe in Pekanbaru

In Pekanbaru, Riau, one local brand that serves cakwe is Lina Cakwe, using the slogan “Teman Ngemil”.
