Texture

Cakwe Texture: Crisp Outside, Light Inside

Texture is one of the most recognizable characteristics of cakwe.

The outside

The exterior is the part that receives direct heat during frying. A well-prepared cakwe can develop a golden-brown, crisp surface with a characteristic fried aroma.

The inside

The interior is generally lighter and more airy. The combination of a crisp outside and lighter inside creates the characteristic contrast when cakwe is bitten.

What can affect texture?

Texture can be influenced by dough composition, dough handling, development time, frying technique, and what happens after the cakwe is cooked.

Why enjoy it warm?

Freshly fried cakwe can have a more noticeable crisp exterior and aroma. Its texture may change as it sits at room temperature.

Texture and dipping sauces

A crisp cakwe can be paired with sauces such as sweet-and-sour sauce, chili sauce, or peanut sauce.
