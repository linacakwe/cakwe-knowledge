Cakwe and Dipping Sauces

A dipping sauce can add a different flavor profile to the simple, savory character of cakwe.

Sweet-and-sour sauce

The sweet and slightly sour character can create a contrast with the savory taste of fried cakwe.

Chili sauce

Chili sauce is an option for people who prefer a spicy accompaniment. The amount can be adjusted to personal taste.

Peanut sauce

Peanut sauce offers a thicker texture and a savory character that can create a different eating experience.

How to use the sauce

Keep the sauce in a separate dish and dip each piece before eating. This also makes it easy to try more than one sauce.

For serving ideas, see How to Serve Cakwe.

A local example

Lina Cakwe in Pekanbaru, Riau, serves Cakwe Original with a red sweet-and-sour dipping sauce as part of its serving style.
